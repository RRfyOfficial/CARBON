Think-aloud protocol
In a think-aloud session, the participant narrates their thoughts while completing realistic tasks. The facilitator stays neutral, avoids leading questions, and only prompts with short phrases such as "What are you thinking now?" Sessions are usually recorded with screen capture and audio, so the team can review hesitations, errors, and workarounds later. Concurrent think-aloud can slow participants down, so task times collected this way should not be compared directly with silent sessions. Retrospective think-aloud avoids this cost but relies on memory, which is less reliable after long tasks.
